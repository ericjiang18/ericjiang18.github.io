I’m <strong>Eric Jiang</strong>, a Ph.D. student in Statistics and Data Science at <a href="https://www.ucla.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCLA</a>, advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fqDBtzYAAAAJ&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Kai-Wei Chang</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=7k_1QFIAAAAJ&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Ying Nian Wu</a>.</p>

      <div id="research-interests">
        <p>My research focuses on advancing <strong>large language models</strong> in <strong>reasoning, safety, and agentic capabilities</strong>, with the goal of building systems that are reliable, effective, and scalable for real-world applications.</p>
        <p>I am currently developing <strong>agent harnesses</strong> for tackling challenging mathematical problems, including open problems. Looking ahead, I aim to design efficient agent infrastructure that improves how LLM-based agents use tools, memory, and long-horizon reasoning.</p>
